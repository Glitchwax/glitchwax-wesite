Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

Glitch Wax ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect, why, and how it's used.

Information We Collect

When you place an order, our payment processor (Square) collects the information needed to process your transaction, such as your name, shipping address, and payment details. We do not store your full payment card information ourselves.

When you contact us through our site or social media, we collect the information you provide, such as your name and email address, in order to respond to you.

How We Use Information

We use order and contact information to fulfill orders, respond to inquiries, and improve our products and marketing. We use aggregated, non-identifying analytics (such as Instagram account insights) to understand how our social media content performs.

Third-Party Services

We use Square to process payments and Meta (Facebook/Instagram) to manage our social media presence and view account-level insights. These services have their own privacy policies governing how they handle data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share information with service providers (such as Square) as necessary to operate our business.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want to request deletion of your information, reach out via our contact page.
